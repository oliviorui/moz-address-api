# Manica urban reconciliation (2025)

This folder stores current INE 2025 territorial tables for the two Manica Province districts that cannot be safely promoted from the historical MozGIS 2017 snapshot.

## Chimoio

The INE 2025 leaflet header declares:

- 3 administrative posts
- 32 localities
- 1 municipality

But the detailed territorial table enumerates:

- 4 urban administrative posts
- 33 bairros

Because the source is internally inconsistent, these records are research-only until the canonical model for Chimoio is reconciled.

## Manica District

The INE 2025 leaflet header declares 18 localities.

The detailed territorial table enumerates:

- 10 bairros in the Municipality of Manica
- 3 localities in Machipanda
- 4 localities in Messica
- 2 localities in Mavonde

That produces 19 named subunits, so the counting convention must be resolved before marking the whole district complete.

The 9 non-municipal localities and the 10 municipal bairros are preserved separately in the research snapshot.

## Baseline discrepancy

The INE Cabo Delgado 2023 provincial baseline reports 12 neighborhoods for
Pemba and 78 neighborhoods province-wide.

The current Pemba municipal source reports 13 neighborhoods, so the 2023
province-wide neighborhood total is retained as historical/statistical context
and must not be treated as the current canonical neighborhood checksum.

## Remaining districts

Do not promote historical neighborhood records for the remaining Cabo Delgado
districts until a current nominal official source has been established for each
urban/municipal layer.

The locality layer (131 localities) and administrative-post layer (56 posts)
remain independently reconciled and canonical.

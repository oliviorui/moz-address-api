# Cabo Delgado administrative posts — resolved

Status: RESOLVED
Resolved: 2026-10-23

## Decision

The canonical district-level structure uses 56 administrative posts.

A 2022 official Electricidade de Moçambique / Government publication explicitly
states that Cabo Delgado had 56 administrative posts. This matches the complete
district-level administrative-post structure reconstructed from INE / official
district publications.

The provincial statistical-yearbook figure of 57 is retained as a documented
source discrepancy and is not used as the canonical district checksum because
it does not reconcile with the district-level structure.

## Canonical rule

- Pemba: 0 district administrative posts in this dataset layer.
- 16 rural districts: 56 administrative posts in total.
- Urban / municipal subdivisions are handled separately from rural district
  administrative posts.
- Locality promotion remains blocked until the reconciliation configuration
  also resolves the current neighborhood layer.

## Evidence

- Official EDM / Government publication (2022): states 56 administrative posts
  existed in Cabo Delgado.
- INE and official district publications: used to cross-check post names and
  district membership.
- INE statistical yearbooks: retained as statistical references, including the
  documented 57-post provincial discrepancy.

# Nacala neighborhoods

Status: BLOCKED_ON_NOMINAL_CURRENT_SOURCE

## Gap

INE, *Anuário Estatístico, Província de Nampula, 2024*, Quadro 1.2.1 (page 4 of the document), reports for the province:

- 72 administrative posts
- 171 localities
- 63 city neighborhoods: Cidade de Nampula 18, Ilha de Moçambique 4, Nacala 41

The dataset has all 72 administrative posts and 171 localities, and the 22 neighborhoods of Cidade de Nampula and Ilha de Moçambique. The 41 neighborhoods of Nacala are missing.

## Historical reference

The INE/MozGIS 2017 layer has 40 unique neighborhood names for Nacala Porto, one fewer than the current count, so the list cannot be promoted by count matching.

## Required evidence before promotion

A current INE or municipal list of Nacala's 41 neighborhoods, or an official explanation of how the 2017 list changed.

The two Nacala Porto administrative posts (Mutiva and Muanona) have no child records until this is resolved.
